Quantum networks distribute entanglement between distant users for secure communication, distributed sensing and distributed quantum computing. Because every entanglement attempt succeeds with a probability that decays with distance, the network relies on switches and repeaters that swap entanglement to connect users without a direct link. The threads below cover how a single switch should be operated and how a whole network should be designed.
