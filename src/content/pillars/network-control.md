Classical networks raise the same questions as quantum ones in a different language: how much traffic a topology can carry, how to schedule and route under uncertainty, and how to share scarce resources among users who do not cooperate. The threads below range from directional wireless networks and network slicing to game-theoretic resource sharing, learning-based scheduling and representation learning on graphs.
