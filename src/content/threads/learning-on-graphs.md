Latent graph models embed nodes in a geometric space and explain edges through distances or inner products. This thread studies what such embeddings can express: spectral occupancy rather than rank as the right notion of capacity, compositional embeddings built on Aitchison geometry, and archetypal generative models whose communities are explainable and identifiable.

Joint work with Nikolaos Nakis and collaborators.
