---
title: Games in satisfaction form
pillar: network-control
order: 3
summary: "Enough instead of maximum: satisfaction equilibria for shared and fragile resources."
---

Classical resource allocation assumes every user maximizes its own utility, and the outcome is a Nash equilibrium. In congested systems a user rarely needs the maximum. It needs enough.

Games in satisfaction form replace maximization with satisfaction constraints. We introduced efficient satisfaction equilibria for power control, designed distributed dynamics that provably reach them, extended the framework to fragile common-pool resources and to risk-aware players, and showed consistent gains over Nash outcomes.
